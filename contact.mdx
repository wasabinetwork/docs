## What to Include in Your First Message

You can keep it short. A useful inquiry usually contains:

- **Your business or organization:** name and website, if one exists
- **Your main challenge:** what is difficult, manual, unreliable, outdated, or missing
- **Your desired outcome:** what you want to work better
- **Relevant context:** current tools, users, stakeholders, or existing processes
- **Timing:** when you would ideally start and any important deadline
- **Budget range (optional):** useful for suggesting an appropriately scoped approach
- **Preferred way to connect:** email or meeting

### Copy-Friendly Inquiry Template

```text
Subject: GAMA Works Project Inquiry

Business / organization:
Website (if any):
Service of interest (or "not sure"):
Current challenge:
Desired outcome:
Current tools or setup (if relevant):
Target timeline:
Budget range (optional):
Preferred next step:

Additional context:
```

## A Few Details That Help Us Route Your Request

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="WebReady">
    The **[GAMA WebReady Bundle](/gama-web-ready-bundle)** is designed for a relatively straightforward business website or blog.

    Helpful details:

    - What your business does and whom it serves
    - Whether you already have a website, domain, or business email
    - Whether you need a website or a blog
    - Your preferred domain names, if available
    - Whether you have a logo, images, and content ready
    - Your preferred monthly or annual plan, if known

    For complex websites, advanced e-commerce, or custom applications, a separate IT Services engagement may be more suitable.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Automation">
    **[Automation & Workflow Design](/automation-workflow-design)** is usually the right path for recurring manual work or disconnected processes.

    Helpful details:

    - What task or workflow repeats
    - Who performs it today and which tools are involved
    - Approximate frequency or volume
    - Where errors, delays, or handoffs occur
    - What needs human approval or judgment
    - The time, cost, or reliability improvement you hope to achieve

    A diagram or simple description of the current workflow is useful, but not required for the first conversation.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="IT Services">
    **[IT Services](/it-services)** is intended for custom or higher-complexity technical work.

    Helpful details:

    - The application, website, platform, or infrastructure involved
    - Existing hosting, cloud, domain, or software environment, if known
    - Whether you need a new build, integration, migration, assessment, or ongoing support
    - Business-critical constraints, dependencies, or delivery milestones
    - Who currently maintains the system
    - What is in scope and what must remain unchanged

    Please describe technical issues without emailing credentials or sensitive access information.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Governance & Funding">
    **[Governance & Funding Systems](/governance-funding-systems)** supports decision-making, grants, funding mechanisms, and ecosystem operations.

    Helpful details:

    - Organization, DAO, foundation, fund, or ecosystem name
    - Current governance or grants operating model
    - Stakeholders and decision-making authority
    - Upcoming funding round, proposal, milestone, or governance decision
    - Where accountability, evaluation, reporting, or coordination is breaking down
    - Whether you need a diagnostic review, program design, hands-on operations, or ongoing advisory

    Do not include confidential applicant information or private treasury access credentials in an introductory message.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to choose a service before contacting you?" defaultOpen>
    No. Tell us what is happening and what you want to improve. We will help identify the closest service or whether a custom project is needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I request a quote for a custom project?">
    Yes. Email an outline of your objectives, current setup, constraints, target timeline, and any useful budget guidance. Pricing and scope for custom work are determined after discovery.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can GAMA Works handle projects involving more than one service?">
    Potentially. A project may combine IT Services, workflow design, and governance or funding operations. We will identify dependencies and scope each workstream before committing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is booking a call the only way to get started?">
    No. You can email [hello@gama.works](mailto:hello@gama.works) first. A meeting may be suggested if it would help clarify the work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will you need credentials or access to review my request?">
    Not for the initial inquiry. If technical assessment or implementation later requires access, permissions and security procedures should be agreed as part of the engagement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How quickly will I receive a response?">
    Response times depend on availability and the nature of the request. We do not promise a universal turnaround time on this page; active-client support commitments are governed by the applicable service terms.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
