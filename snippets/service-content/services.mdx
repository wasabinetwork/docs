### 2. Automation & Workflow Design

**Spend less time on repeatable administration and more time on valuable work.**

Designed for teams dealing with fragmented tools, handoff delays, repeated data entry, inconsistent follow-ups, or processes that depend on one person remembering every step.

**Potential work includes:**

- Mapping the current workflow and identifying sources of friction
- Automating repeatable tasks, routing, reminders, and approvals
- Connecting existing business applications
- Adding AI-assisted classification, drafting, or analysis where appropriate
- Improving dashboards, documentation, exception handling, and human review

**A strong fit when:** a recurring process is sufficiently understood, measurable, and worth improving.

**Another service may fit better when:** your main need is a new custom application or a broader infrastructure migration.

**Make important decisions clearer, resource allocation more accountable, and operations easier to trust.**

## How the Services Work Together

We treat the four offers as connected but distinct.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Establish Your Digital Presence">
    WebReady helps a business become easier to find, understand, and contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Improve the Work Behind the Website">
    Automation can help connect requests, customer communications, internal workflows, and reporting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build What Standard Tools Cannot Cover">
    IT Services addresses specialized applications, integrations, infrastructure, and technical ownership needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Shared Decisions and Funding Accountable">
    Governance & Funding Systems establishes clear processes when resources, stakeholders, and decision authority require more structure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This is **not a mandatory sequence**. Many clients need only one service, and not every organization needs a governance engagement. We recommend the most appropriate starting point.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if I do not know which service I need?" defaultOpen>
    Describe the problem through the [Contact page](/contact) or [book a conversation](https://cal.com/wasabi/gama). We can help identify whether WebReady, an assessment, automation, a custom technical project, or governance support is the best fit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I start with a smaller project?">
    Yes. In many cases, a clearly bounded starting point provides more value than a large and ambiguous transformation. The initial scope depends on your requirements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does GAMA Works work with existing tools and systems?">
    Yes, where appropriate. Improving and connecting existing systems may be more practical than replacing them. Technical constraints and integration permissions must be assessed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a project include multiple services?">
    Potentially. For example, custom software may support a grants program, or automation may complement existing technical infrastructure. The workstreams and responsibilities are scoped separately or together as appropriate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are there fixed prices for all services?">
    No. WebReady has published monthly and annual plans. Custom technical, automation, and governance engagements require discovery and a defined proposal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every service include ongoing support?">
    No. Support, maintenance, and operational availability depend on the product or project agreement. The applicable service page or scoped proposal explains the relevant terms.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do you guarantee leads, business revenue, or funding approvals?">
    No. We focus on clearly scoped systems and processes that support better operations. Website rankings, leads, revenues, grant awards, and voting outcomes depend on factors beyond the engagement.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
