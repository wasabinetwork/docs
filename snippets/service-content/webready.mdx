## Source Files and Ownership

WebReady is intentionally structured to keep the initial cost low while giving committed clients a clear path to ownership.

### Yearly Clients

Clients who enter and complete a **12-month term**, keep the account in good standing, and pay all amounts due can receive the transferable source files created specifically for their WebReady project.

### Monthly Clients

Monthly subscriptions do not include source-file transfer until the account reaches **12 months of paid subscription age** and remains in good standing.

If source files are needed sooner, GAMA Works may provide a separate source-file buyout quote.

### What Does Not Transfer

GAMA Works retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property, including reusable templates, internal tools, frameworks, workflows, libraries, and proprietary systems. Third-party software and licensed assets remain subject to their respective licenses.

<Note>
  Client-provided branding, content, business information, and other client-owned materials remain the client's property.
</Note>
